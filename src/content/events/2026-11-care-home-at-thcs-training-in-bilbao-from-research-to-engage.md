On November 9, 2026, Care@Home was represented at the "From Research to Engagement" training event in Bilbao, Spain, organized within the European Partnership on Transforming Health and Care Systems (THCS). The event brought together coordinators and representatives of THCS-funded projects to explore how research outcomes can be effectively communicated to the public, policymakers and other stakeholders.

The Care@Home project was represented by Prof. Dr. Agnė Paulauskaitė-Tarasevičienė and Dr. Rytis Augustauskas from Kaunas University of Technology (KTU). Throughout the training, participants explored practical approaches to science communication, engaged in interactive activities and exchanged experiences with researchers from other international projects. The event also introduced the THCS Knowledge Hub, a platform designed to support knowledge sharing and increase the visibility of research outcomes.

For Care@Home, the training provided a valuable opportunity to present the project's vision of developing an AI-powered, contactless monitoring system to support safer and more independent living for older adults. The project combines smart sensor technologies, artificial intelligence and privacy-conscious data processing to explore innovative approaches to elderly care without unnecessarily disrupting everyday life.

Beyond sharing research experiences, the event highlighted the importance of making scientific developments accessible and meaningful to society. The knowledge gained and connections established in Bilbao will support Care@Home in communicating its research, engaging stakeholders and strengthening collaboration within the international health and care innovation community.

The visit marked another valuable step in connecting Care@Home's technological research with real-world healthcare needs, supporting the project's broader goal of improving quality of life and promoting independent living for older adults.
