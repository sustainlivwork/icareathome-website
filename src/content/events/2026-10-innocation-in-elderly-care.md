On October 20–21, 2026, Care@Home was represented at a study visit to Berlin, organized by the German-Baltic Chamber of Commerce (AHK). The visit brought together 15 participants from the Baltic States to explore innovative solutions in elderly care and learn how Germany is addressing the challenges of an ageing society.

Throughout the two-day visit, participants had the opportunity to discover how artificial intelligence, robotics, digital health technologies and smart living solutions are being integrated into elderly care. Meetings with German companies and experts provided valuable insights into practical applications of these technologies and their potential to support both older adults and care professionals.

One of the highlights was meeting Willi, the social robot developed by Navel Robotics and learning more about the role of social robotics in supporting older adults. The visit also offered opportunities to exchange experiences, discuss emerging technologies, and explore possibilities for future collaboration. For Care@Home, this experience was particularly valuable, as it closely aligns with the project's mission to develop innovative, technology-driven solutions that support independent living, enhance well-being, and improve the quality of elderly care. Seeing these innovations in practice provided fresh perspectives and reinforced the importance of combining technological advancement with a human-centered approach.

The visit was an inspiring opportunity to gain new knowledge, build international connections and bring valuable ideas into the continued development of Care@Home. Special thanks to the AHK for organizing the visit and bringing together professionals dedicated to shaping the future of elderly care.
