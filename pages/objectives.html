Project Objectives

Care@Home aims to develop a comprehensive, AI-driven ecosystem for contactless monitoring, enabling safer and more independent living for older adults.

Monitoring

Identify the most relevant daily activities, physical states, and environmental factors that reflect the health status and independence level of older adults.

Define clinically relevant vital signs suitable for continuous, contactless monitoring.

Technology

Develop an AI-powered home care system that detects assistance needs and monitors autonomy in daily routines using Nearable & Airable Smart Sensors (NASS).

Design and implement advanced AI algorithms and predictive models for early detection of health risks and behavioral changes.

Ethics & Privacy

Build a responsible framework for sensitive data monitoring that prioritizes user trust, privacy, and ethical AI principles.

Implement secure local data processing and privacy-preserving techniques to transform sensitive data into non-sensitive representations.

Validation

Evaluate system performance and adaptability across diverse populations of older adults with varying health conditions and living environments.

Ensure high accuracy, usability, and acceptance of the system through real-world testing and user-centered validation.

Together, these objectives form the foundation for a scalable, ethical, and impactful AI solution that supports independent living and strengthens healthcare systems across Europe.
