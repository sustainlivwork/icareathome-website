About the Project

Understanding the need, purpose, and impact of Care@Home.

Background & Need

Europe is experiencing a rapid demographic shift. The population aged 65 and over is growing significantly and is expected to reach nearly 30% by 2050. At the same time, a large proportion of older adults live alone, increasing the risk of social isolation and delayed medical response.

Healthcare systems are under increasing pressure, with a projected shortage of millions of healthcare professionals in the coming years. Traditional care models are becoming difficult to sustain, especially for continuous monitoring and support at home.

The Problem

Existing monitoring solutions, such as wearables, often fail in real-life scenarios due to usability issues, limited adoption, and lack of scalability. Many systems are intrusive, require active user engagement, or do not integrate well into everyday life.

As a result, early signs of health deterioration frequently go unnoticed, leading to preventable hospitalizations and reduced quality of life for older adults.

Project Goal

Care@Home aims to develop a cutting-edge AI-based, contactless monitoring ecosystem that enables continuous, non-invasive observation of individuals in their home environments.

The system integrates IoT sensors, TinyML, and privacy-preserving technologies to detect assistance needs, monitor daily activities, and track vital signs without disrupting everyday life.

Vision

“Invisible care and real impact.”

Care@Home envisions a future where AI supports independent living by working seamlessly in the background—empowering older adults, supporting caregivers, and enabling timely, data-driven interventions without compromising privacy or dignity.

Expected Impact

For Older Adults

Improved safety, independence, and quality of life through continuous, unobtrusive monitoring.

For Caregivers & Families

Peace of mind and reduced burden through real-time insights and early alerts.

For Healthcare Systems

Reduced workload and costs by enabling preventive care and minimizing unnecessary hospitalizations.

For Society

Scalable, ethical AI solutions that address demographic challenges and support sustainable healthcare systems.
